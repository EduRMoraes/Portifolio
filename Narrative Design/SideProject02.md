# Antiga Alameda

> A worldbuilding project created for a tabletop RPG, focused on environmental storytelling, biomechanical horror, exploration, and location design.

## Overview

**Antiga Alameda** is a worldbuilding project developed for a tabletop RPG campaign.

The setting takes place in the coastal city of **Santa Verônica**, particularly within the devastated region of Alameda.

Once a large urban and coastal district, Alameda has been transformed by the spread of an **infectious biomechanical technology** that has merged with the surrounding ecosystem.

The result is a constantly changing environment where urban structures, organic matter, technology, and nature have become increasingly difficult to distinguish.

The setting was designed around **exploration, environmental horror, and emergent storytelling**.

---

# Setting

Alameda was once a major urban district containing residential neighborhoods, commercial centers, industrial facilities, and a large coastal area.

After the collapse, several factors transformed the region:

- Rising sea levels
- Unstable weather patterns
- Coastal ecosystem expansion
- Biomechanical infection
- Human experimentation
- Infrastructure collapse

Nature has reclaimed large portions of the coastline, while the infection continues to spread through the urban environment.

The result is a region divided into distinct but interconnected sectors.

# Major Areas

## Coastal Region

The former coastline has been consumed by mud, mangroves, contaminated water, and biomechanical growth.

### Mud Coast

A viscous mixture of sand, concrete, shells, and biological fibers.

Biomechanical structures emerge from the mud, while amphibious creatures adapted to the infection inhabit the area.

### Dissolved Breakwater

The former breakwater has been eroded and reshaped into an unstable corridor.

The area focuses on environmental horror, strange sounds, and encounters with entities originating from the water.

### Santa Verônica Lighthouse

An abandoned lighthouse partially consumed by the infection.

Its light projects strange geometric patterns through the surrounding fog, while the interior appears to react to sound and physical contact.

---

# Miragalha Mangrove

The mangrove has become deeply integrated with the biomechanical infection.

### Conductive Roots

Trees have merged with organic cables that behave like exposed nerves.

Movement through the mud can trigger signals that travel through the mangrove and alert larger creatures.

### Iron Creek

A brackish waterway where the infection has transformed the surface into thin metallic structures.

Strange circuit-like formations can be found beneath the water.

### Abandoned IBTMA Station

A former marine and atmospheric technology research facility.

Research records suggest that the infection may be capable of interpreting and reproducing environmental and weather patterns.

---

# Belmonte Urban Center

The central urban region contains old buildings, commercial districts, and modern infrastructure partially consumed by the infection.

### Caldeira Avenue

Once a major commercial street.

Shops, signs, buildings, and infrastructure have been absorbed into biomechanical structures.

### Arcadia Tower

A forty-story skyscraper whose upper floors no longer follow the original architecture.

The lower floors remain partially intact, while the upper levels have become increasingly organic and structurally impossible.

### Old Market

A former underground market transformed into a biomechanical hive.

The area can be used for encounters involving survivors, hostile groups, and communities that have adapted to the increasingly abnormal environment.

---

# Residential Sectors

These areas represent some of the remaining human settlements.

### Cantareira District

A residential neighborhood where the mangrove has begun entering gardens and streets.

Some inhabitants have developed minor physical adaptations to the infection.

### Nova Alameda

A high-rise residential district containing both surviving buildings and structures partially consumed by the infection.

Some groups secretly study the phenomenon here.

### Perimeter Square

A former public park transformed into a contaminated saltwater basin.

A large biomechanical monolith has emerged from its center.

---

# Industrial Zone

The industrial district played a significant role in the collapse of Alameda.

### Memorial Pier

A former cargo and unloading facility.

Containers have transformed into large biological structures, while abandoned machinery appears to operate without human intervention.

### Maritime Thermomechanical Plant

A power facility partially integrated into the biomechanical organism.

Its interior resembles a mechanical circulatory system, with pipes functioning like enormous arteries.

### Warehouse 17

A location used by displaced human factions.

Improvised settlements and biological barricades have transformed the warehouse into one of the few organized human-controlled areas.

---

# Natural Collapse Zones

Areas where environmental instability has become part of the geography itself.

### The Rift

A deep geological rupture created by repeated micro-tsunami events.

Electrical discharges occur throughout the area, while biomechanical structures continue growing along its walls.

### Spectral Salt Lake

A contaminated body of water characterized by sudden evaporation.

The resulting vapor produces unusual geometric patterns, accompanied by reports of enormous shapes beneath the surface.

### Ruptor Hills

A mountainous region permanently affected by violent storms.

The vegetation behaves strangely, leading some survivors to believe that the local weather patterns may possess a form of intelligence.

# Worldbuilding Goals

The setting was designed around several principles:

- **Environmental storytelling**
- **Exploration**
- **Biomechanical horror**
- **Post-apocalyptic worldbuilding**
- **Distinct location identities**
- **Human survival and adaptation**
- **Unstable ecosystems**
- **Mystery and discovery**

Each location was designed to provide more than visual atmosphere.

Its geography, inhabitants, hazards, and environmental conditions can be used to generate **encounters, quests, discoveries, character interactions, and narrative events** during a tabletop campaign.

---

# Design Philosophy

The central idea behind **Antiga Alameda** is the collision between:

**Nature + Technology + Human Civilization**

The setting explores what happens when these three systems stop existing independently.

Buildings become organisms.

Forests become networks.

Machines become biological.

Weather becomes unpredictable.

And the environment itself becomes part of the threat.
