# Quest Concept — Narrative Design

This project is a **Quest Concept** created to demonstrate my narrative design, quest development, branching narrative, and worldbuilding skills for a **Narrative Designer** position.

The concept was created from scratch using the existing setting as a foundation, primarily considering the established factions of **The Untainted** and **The Scavengers**, alongside selected ideas and unused concepts found in the game's cut content.

Rather than adapting an existing quest, I used these elements as narrative constraints and developed an original story around them. The quest explores the relationship between the Untainted and Elyseum's **Black Market**, gradually expanding a seemingly straightforward fuel recovery mission into an investigation involving drug trafficking, human trafficking, faction interests, and the wider criminal economy operating beneath the city's visible society.

The project focuses on **branching narrative and player choice**, with the central decision determining how the protagonist, Eli, interacts with the Untainted and the Black Market in subsequent quests. One path prioritizes the original contract and establishes Eli as a reliable professional within the criminal network, while the other leads him to intervene and expose a human trafficking operation, creating a different relationship with the factions and opening a separate narrative path.

The concept also explores themes of **human commodification, complicity, moral disengagement, criminal economies, and the consequences of treating people as commodities**. The Untainted are portrayed not simply as a conventional antagonist faction, but as one visible component of a larger Black Market whose participants range from street-level criminals to potentially influential figures within Elyseum.

The complete quest concept, including its narrative structure, branching choices, faction relationships, themes, and future quest directions, was developed as a **mind map in FigJam**.
