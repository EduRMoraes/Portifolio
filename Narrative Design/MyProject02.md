# 1969

> An original supernatural mystery story exploring souls, karma, identity, and extraordinary abilities.

## Overview

**1969** is an original supernatural mystery story that begins with a mysterious incident involving astronauts and the inhabitants of the Moon.

The story then shifts to the present day, where a group of individuals with supernatural abilities becomes involved in a world marked by political instability, crime, violence, and conflicts surrounding the nature of the human soul.

Unlike conventional superpowers, the abilities in this world are directly connected to the **soul and the karma accumulated throughout a person's life**.

---

# Story Structure

## 1969 — The Moon

The story begins with a mysterious event:

Astronauts are attacked by unknown inhabitants of the Moon.

The incident becomes the first major indication that humanity's understanding of the Moon may be fundamentally incomplete.

---

## Earth — Present Day

Years later, the story moves to Earth.

The main characters are introduced alongside the supernatural system governing their abilities.

The world is experiencing significant political and social instability, creating an environment where individuals with supernatural powers can influence conflicts far beyond ordinary crime.

The story gradually introduces:

- The protagonists
- The antagonists
- Supporting characters
- The supernatural power system
- The political state of the world
- The relationship between souls and karma

---

# The Power System

The abilities of each character are connected to their **soul and karma**.

A person's experiences, actions, emotions, and moral choices influence the nature of their soul.

Their supernatural ability is therefore not simply a random power.

It represents something about **who they are**.

This creates a system where powers can evolve alongside their users.
