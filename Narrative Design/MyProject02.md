# 1969

> An original supernatural mystery story exploring souls, karma, identity, and extraordinary abilities.

## Overview

**1969** is an original supernatural mystery story that begins with a mysterious incident involving astronauts and the inhabitants of the Moon.

The story then shifts to the present day, where a group of individuals with supernatural abilities becomes involved in a world marked by political instability, crime, violence, and conflicts surrounding the nature of the human soul.

Unlike conventional superpowers, the abilities in this world are directly connected to the **soul and the karma accumulated throughout a person's life**.

---

# Story Structure

## 1969 — The Moon

The story begins with a mysterious event:

Astronauts are attacked by unknown inhabitants of the Moon.

The incident becomes the first major indication that humanity's understanding of the Moon may be fundamentally incomplete.

---

## Earth — Present Day

Years later, the story moves to Earth.

The main characters are introduced alongside the supernatural system governing their abilities.

The world is experiencing significant political and social instability, creating an environment where individuals with supernatural powers can influence conflicts far beyond ordinary crime.

The story gradually introduces:

- The protagonists
- The antagonists
- Supporting characters
- The supernatural power system
- The political state of the world
- The relationship between souls and karma

---

# The Power System

The abilities of each character are connected to their **soul and karma**.

A person's experiences, actions, emotions, and moral choices influence the nature of their soul.

Their supernatural ability is therefore not simply a random power.

# First Major Arc — The Ruler

The first major arc introduces the central conflict between **justice, revenge, exploitation, and the value of human life**.

## The Protagonist

At the beginning of the story, the protagonist does not yet possess the energy-infused wires that will eventually become one of his defining abilities.

His initial ability is based on a **Retribution Contract**.

### Retribution Contract

The protagonist can establish an invisible agreement between himself and another individual.

If the target causes harm, that harm is returned to them with increased intensity.

However, the contract is not without consequences.

**The protagonist is bound by the same rules.**

If he makes a mistake or targets the wrong person, he must bear the consequences himself.

This establishes an important principle of his power:

> **Justice must remain balanced, even when it hurts the person enforcing it.**

---

## The Evolution of the Ability

The invisible contracts eventually begin manifesting as **connections between souls**.

These connections gradually evolve into the energy-infused wires that become the protagonist's signature ability.

This transformation occurs after a major turning point.

The **Leader of the Moon** kills the protagonist's intended target in his place, causing his soul to temporarily shatter and become fragmented.

The experience forces him to confront the consequences of his pursuit of revenge.

Instead of continuing to seek retribution for those who are already gone, he begins pursuing **reconstruction**.

His motivation changes from:

> **Punishing those responsible for what was lost.**

to:

> **Protecting the lives that can still be saved.**

This becomes one of the protagonist's defining character developments.

---

# The First Villain — The Ruler

The first major antagonist is a powerful figure capable of manipulating politicians and large portions of the population.

His abilities demonstrate how the soul can be exploited as a resource, while his actions expose the corruption and greed present within the political system.

## Soul Economy

The Ruler creates an invisible system of supernatural exchange.

Individuals can surrender portions of their souls in exchange for:

- Political success
- Protection
- Healing
- Power
- Social influence

The more a person receives, the greater their **existential debt** becomes.

Politicians enter these agreements believing they remain in control, while the population gradually becomes dependent on the system without fully understanding its consequences.

The souls collected through these agreements become the Ruler's source of power.

---

## Asymmetric Contract

The Ruler's central ability mirrors the protagonist's own power, but with a fundamental difference.

**The protagonist represents balanced justice.**

**The Ruler represents exploitation.**

He can manipulate the terms of his contracts without the victim fully understanding them.

He can:

- Define hidden conditions
- Demand more than he originally offered
- Collect payment through life, sanity, or identity
- Manipulate individuals into believing they willingly accepted the agreement
