# Narrative & Storytelling

## Overview

Beyond programming and technical development, I have a strong and long-standing interest in **narrative, storytelling, and worldbuilding**.

For several years, I have been writing and developing stories of my own, creating characters, settings, concepts, and chapters that I continue to revisit and expand over time.

This personal practice has gradually shaped my interest in understanding **how stories are constructed, how characters evolve, and how fictional worlds can feel coherent and alive**.

## Areas of Interest

My interests within narrative development include:

- **Worldbuilding** — creating coherent worlds, cultures, environments, and internal rules
- **Character Development** — building characters with motivations, conflicts, relationships, and progression
- **Environmental Storytelling** — communicating narrative through locations, objects, visual details, and atmosphere
- **Narrative Structure** — developing plots, arcs, pacing, conflicts, and progression
- **Dialogue** — using conversations to establish character, relationships, information, and tone
- **Character Relationships** — exploring how interactions and conflicts shape both characters and the story
- **Atmosphere & Tone** — using setting, pacing, visuals, and sound to establish emotional context
- **Story-Driven Game Design** — integrating narrative directly into gameplay rather than treating it as a separate layer

## Storytelling Through Games

What particularly interests me about games is that they are an **interactive storytelling medium**.

A story does not have to exist only through dialogue, cutscenes, or written text. It can also emerge through:

- Gameplay mechanics
- Level design
- Environmental details
- Character behavior
- Visual design
- Player choices
- Exploration
- Progression systems
- Sound and atmosphere

The player can discover a story rather than simply being told one.

This is one of the main reasons I became interested in combining my **technical background in software development** with **game development and narrative design**.

My long-term goal is to continue developing both sides of this skill set:


I see narrative design as an opportunity to connect **technology, gameplay, characters, worldbuilding, and player experience** into a cohesive whole.
