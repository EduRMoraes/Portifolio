# Game World & Lore

The game takes place in a **post-apocalyptic cyberpunk world** shaped by decades of technological advancement, environmental collapse, political conflict, and human exploitation.

Humanity developed technologies capable of transforming almost every aspect of life.

Instead of using that technology responsibly, societies increasingly prioritized economic growth, political power, and corporate interests.

Eventually, the systems created to improve human life became part of the reason the world began to collapse.

---

## The World

The player is placed inside a massive **Mega Building**, one of the remaining structures capable of sustaining human life after the collapse of the outside world.

The building functions as more than a setting.

It is a representation of the world itself.

Different floors, sectors, inhabitants, and environments reveal different consequences of humanity's past decisions.

Outside the building, the world has been devastated by environmental and political collapse.

Inside, humanity continues to survive — but the same social problems that contributed to the collapse have not disappeared.

---

# Environmental Storytelling

The narrative is not primarily delivered through traditional cutscenes.

Instead, the player discovers the history of the world through its environment and the people living within it.

Storytelling is distributed across:

- NPC dialogue
- Personal diaries
- Newspapers
- Documents
- Abandoned rooms
- Environmental details
- Propaganda
- Advertisements
- Graffiti
- Damaged technology
- Conversations between survivors

The goal is to allow players to gradually reconstruct what happened rather than presenting the entire history directly.

A newspaper might explain a political crisis.

A damaged apartment might reveal how an ordinary family experienced it.

An NPC might remember an event differently.

A discarded corporate document might reveal information that contradicts the official narrative.

The world therefore tells its story through **fragments**.

---

# Themes

The game's worldbuilding explores the consequences of unchecked technological and economic development.

Major themes include:

- Environmental collapse
- Global warming
- Deforestation
- Resource exploitation
- Political conflict
- Social segregation
- Corporate influence
- Economic inequality
- Technological dependence
- Misuse of advanced technology
- Human survival
- The consequences of greed

The intention is not to present technology itself as the enemy.

Instead, the world explores what happens when **human ambition develops faster than human responsibility**.

---

# The Mega Building

The Mega Building serves as the primary setting of the game.

It was originally designed as a self-sufficient urban structure capable of housing large populations while providing access to advanced technologies, commerce, healthcare, security, and essential infrastructure.

After the collapse, however, the building became increasingly isolated from the outside world.

Different floors developed their own social structures, problems, and methods of survival.

As the player progresses through the building, they encounter different manifestations of the world's collapse.

Some areas may remain relatively functional.

Others may have been abandoned.

Some may be controlled by factions.

Others may have become dangerous environments where technology and nature have begun to interact in unexpected ways.

---

# A World Told Through Its People

The inhabitants of the Mega Building are an important part of the narrative.

Each NPC has their own experience of the collapse.

Some remember the world before it deteriorated.

Others were born after the collapse and know the old world only through stories.

This creates different perspectives on the same events.

A politician may describe a historical event as necessary.

A survivor may remember it as a tragedy.

A corporate employee may believe the technology saved millions of lives.

Another character may have lost their entire family because of it.

The player is therefore encouraged to form their own understanding of the world.

---

# The Collapse

The collapse was not caused by a single event.

It was the result of decades of interconnected problems.

Technological advancement accelerated economic and industrial development, while political and corporate interests increasingly prioritized short-term gains.

Environmental degradation intensified.

Forests disappeared.

Global temperatures rose.

Resources became increasingly scarce.

Political tensions escalated.

Social segregation became more pronounced.

And advanced technologies originally created to solve these problems were increasingly used to control, exploit, or profit from them.

Eventually, the systems humanity had built became impossible to sustain.

The apocalypse was not the end of civilization in a single moment.

It was the consequence of **many decisions accumulating over time**.

---

# Narrative Direction

The game's narrative aims to make the player question the world rather than simply explain it.

The player should gradually realize that the ruined environment is not simply a backdrop for the game.

**Every damaged structure, abandoned object, newspaper, NPC, and technological system exists because something happened before the player arrived.**

The world is therefore designed to function as a narrative layer alongside the gameplay.

> **The player does not simply explore the world. They reconstruct it.**
