# Game Development

## Overview

Game Development is one of my main areas of personal study and experimentation.

I currently develop with **Unity and C#**, working on a **2D survival game** that combines gameplay programming, systems design, visual direction, and narrative concepts.

My approach focuses on understanding not only how individual mechanics are implemented, but also how they interact to create a consistent and engaging gameplay experience.

## Current Focus

### Gameplay Systems

- **Player Movement & Physics** — movement, jumping, acceleration, and responsive controls
- **Combat Systems** — melee combat, damage handling, and combat interactions
- **Enemy AI** — detection, pursuit, combat behavior, and state-based decision making
- **Health & Damage** — reusable health systems, damage handling, and death states
- **Animation Systems** — connecting gameplay states with character animations
- **Environmental Interaction** — interaction between the player, enemies, and the game world

### Software Architecture

- **Modular Systems** — designing independent and reusable gameplay components
- **Game State Management** — managing gameplay states and system interactions
- **Event-Driven Communication** — reducing unnecessary dependencies between systems
- **Debugging & Iteration** — identifying issues, testing solutions, and continuously refining gameplay

## Development Approach

My goal is not simply to implement individual mechanics, but to understand **how different systems communicate and depend on each other**.

While developing, I focus on:

- Writing maintainable and reusable code
- Keeping systems modular and decoupled
- Understanding the impact of architectural decisions
- Iterating through testing and debugging
- Balancing technical structure with gameplay requirements
- Designing systems that can evolve as the project grows
