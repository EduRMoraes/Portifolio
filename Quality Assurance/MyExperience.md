# Quality Assurance

## Overview

My professional experience is primarily focused on **Quality Assurance and software validation**, with an emphasis on ensuring software quality, reliability, and alignment with business and user requirements.

## Areas of Experience

- **Functional Testing** — validating features and system behavior against requirements
- **Feature Validation** — verifying whether new functionality meets its intended purpose
- **Bug Identification & Tracking** — identifying, analyzing, documenting, and following up on defects
- **Test Planning & Documentation** — structuring test scenarios, plans, and supporting documentation
- **Acceptance Criteria** — defining and validating criteria for feature acceptance
- **Development Reviews** — collaborating with development teams during reviews and validation cycles
- **Process Analysis & Improvement** — analyzing workflows and identifying opportunities for improvement
- **Stakeholder & Client Communication** — communicating requirements, findings, issues, and improvements
- **Technical Documentation** — creating and maintaining structured technical and process documentation

## Quality Approach

I'm particularly interested in understanding software from both a **technical and user perspective**.

For me, Quality Assurance goes beyond determining whether a feature works. It involves understanding:

- Whether the feature behaves as intended
- Whether it meets its requirements and acceptance criteria
- How it affects other parts of the system
- How users interact with it
- Whether potential edge cases have been considered
- Whether the overall experience is consistent and reliable
